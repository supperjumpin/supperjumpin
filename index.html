Research notes used for these riffs

  • Components V2: Discord now supports richer message layouts: containers, sections, media galleries, thumbnails, files, buttons, selects, and file upload in modals.
  • Activities: Discord Activities are iframe web apps using the Embedded App SDK, built for social/multiplayer experiences on desktop, mobile, and web.
  • Best-in-class references: Midjourney for public artifact streams, Starboard bots for emergent highlights, event bots for RSVP/reminder ceremony, and Discord Activities case studies for shared play surfaces.
  • Round 3 synthesis: Native components excel at commitment and status because they stay in the chat stream with low friction. Activities excel at the reveal because they provide synchronized shared state, rich layout, live reactions, and recap composition.
  • Round 7: Riff 3 had kids: Three child variants branched from Round 6 Riff 4 (Hybrid Loreboard). Each child shows parentage in the UI: branch badge in the header, family tags on bot messages, and a parent link. The variants explore neon big-card styling, native Discord message-card shapes with slow course reveals, and a sealed-vault build-up that bursts into the hybrid stream.
  • Round 6: ...and now with sparkles: Two conservative riffs keep the current native card language; three riffs go bright and artistic with large rounded cards, glowing orbs, neon numbers, and color-coded eyebrows (pink, yellow, indigo) inspired by the original Variation B/C presentation.
  • Round 5: make the bot interesting: Individual bot messages were rewritten with more voice: RSVPs as place settings, sealed evidence as cold storage, casino roulette, and judged exhibit halls.
