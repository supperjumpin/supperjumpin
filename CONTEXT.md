     1|# Supperjumpin
     2|
     3|Supperjumpin is a social game about inventing and performing absurd food-location combinations inspired by Jon Bois' Supperjumpin article. The game borrows the spirit of the article without treating its exact terminology or rules as binding canon.
     4|
     5|## Language
     6|
     7|**Jump**:
     8|A playable Supperjumpin attempt centered on taking food associated with one place and consuming or presenting it in another place. A **Jump** may be planned, performed, documented, and socially judged.
     9|_Avoid_: Jump, challenge, mission, post
    10|
    11|**Source**:
    12|The place, brand, cuisine, event, or context the food in a **Jump** is associated with.
    13|_Avoid_: Restaurant A, origin
    14|
    15|**Destination**:
    16|The place, brand, venue, or context where the food in a **Jump** is consumed, displayed, or documented.
    17|_Avoid_: Restaurant B, target
    18|
    19|**Food**:
    20|The item or items carried from a **Source** into a **Destination** as part of a **Jump**.
    21|_Avoid_: Meal, order
    22|
    23|**Idea**:
    24|A not-yet-committed **Jump** concept that may be discussed or refined before a **Player** decides to perform it.
    25|_Avoid_: Draft, prompt
    26|
    27|**Planned Jump**:
    28|A **Jump** a **Player** intends to perform and has made visible to a **Group** before submitting **Evidence**.
    29|_Avoid_: Challenge, assignment
    30|
    31|**Performed Jump**:
    32|A **Jump** with submitted **Evidence** claiming that the performance happened.
    33|_Avoid_: Completed jump
    34|
    35|**Judged Jump**:
    36|A **Performed Jump** that has received scores from its **Group**.
    37|_Avoid_: Rated jump, scored jump
    38|
    39|**Off-Season Jump**:
    40|A **Jump** that belongs to a **Group** but does not contribute to **Season** **Standings**.
    41|_Avoid_: Casual jump
    42|
    43|**Judge**:
    44|A **Player** who scores another **Player's** **Performed Jump** within a **Group**. The performer of a **Jump** is not a **Judge** for that **Jump**.
    45|_Avoid_: Voter, reviewer, rater
    46|
    47|**Judging Window**:
    48|The period when a **Performed Jump** can receive **Judgments** from eligible **Judges**.
    49|_Avoid_: Voting period, review window
    50|
    51|**Judgment**:
    52|A **Judge's** submitted scores for a **Performed Jump**.
    53|_Avoid_: Vote, review, rating
    54|
    55|**Final Score**:
    56|The aggregate score for a **Judged Jump** after its **Judging Window** closes, used to update **Standings**.
    57|_Avoid_: Total score
    58|
    59|**Season Score**:
    60|The accumulated score a **Player** earns in a **Season** from non-disqualified **Judged Jumps**.
    61|_Avoid_: Points, rating
    62|
    63|**Mission**:
    64|A non-competitive prompt or objective a **Player** can complete for progression or rewards outside **Season Score**. **Missions** may teach the game, encourage participation, or suggest themed **Jumps** without affecting **Standings**.
    65|_Avoid_: Quest, achievement, task
    66|
    67|**Action Mission**:
    68|A **Mission** completed through app behavior rather than performing a **Jump**.
    69|_Avoid_: Task
    70|
    71|**Jump Mission**:
    72|A **Mission** completed by performing a **Jump** with **Evidence**.
    73|_Avoid_: Solo jump
    74|
    75|**Prompt**:
    76|A reusable **Jump** idea, theme, or constraint that can inspire **Ideas**, **Missions**, or **Bounties**.
    77|_Avoid_: Template, card, challenge
    78|
    79|**Bounty**:
    80|A **Mission** with a meaningful reward for completing a **Prompt**. **Bounties** do not affect **Season Score** by default.
    81|_Avoid_: Prize challenge
    82|
    83|**Sponsored Bounty**:
    84|A **Bounty** funded or promoted by an external sponsor, such as a restaurant or brand. **Sponsored Bounties** do not affect **Season Score** by default.
    85|_Avoid_: Ad, promotion
    86|
    87|**Unwitnessed Jump**:
    88|A **Performed Jump** whose **Judging Window** closed without any submitted **Judgments**, so it does not affect **Standings**.
    89|_Avoid_: Unrated jump
    90|
    91|**Dispute**:
    92|A **Player-raised** challenge that a **Performed Jump** may not satisfy **House Rules**, **Credibility**, or claimed **Source**, **Destination**, or **Food**.
    93|_Avoid_: Report, flag, appeal
    94|
    95|**Disqualified Jump**:
    96|A **Performed Jump** removed from **Standings** by a **Season Commissioner** or **Group Admin** because it failed **House Rules** or another competition requirement.
    97|_Avoid_: Deleted jump, rejected jump
    98|
    99|**Removed Jump**:
   100|A **Jump** hidden from normal Group visibility because of a serious safety, privacy, legal, or platform violation. **Removed Jumps** are distinct from **Disqualified Jumps**, which may remain visible while not affecting **Standings**.
   101|_Avoid_: Deleted jump
   102|
   103|**Player**:
   104|A person who participates in Supperjumpin by planning, performing, viewing, or judging **Jumps**.
   105|_Avoid_: User, member, athlete
   106|
   107|**Level**:
   108|A non-competitive progression marker earned by a **Player** through **Missions** and participation. **Levels** are separate from **Season Score** and **Standings**.
   109|_Avoid_: Rank, currency
   110|
   111|**Account**:
   112|The login identity that owns a **Player**. **Account** identity is separate from in-game **Player** identity so login methods can change without redefining play history.
   113|_Avoid_: User
   114|
   115|**Group**:
   116|A bounded set of **Players** who share, judge, and compare **Jumps** with each other. Supperjumpin play is primarily scoped to **Groups**, not a global feed.
   117|_Avoid_: League, community, club
   118|
   119|**Group Membership**:
   120|The relationship between a **Player** and a **Group**, including that **Player's** participation status or role within the **Group**.
   121|_Avoid_: Membership, subscription
   122|
   123|**Group Admin**:
   124|A **Player** with durable authority over a **Group**, including emergency override authority over an **Active Season**.
   125|_Avoid_: Owner, moderator
   126|
   127|**Invite**:
   128|A way for a **Player** to bring another person into a **Group**.
   129|_Avoid_: Invitation link, referral
   130|
   131|**Season**:
   132|A bounded competition period within a **Group** where **Judged Jumps** contribute to standings and awards. A **Group** has at most one active or closing **Season** at a time.
   133|_Avoid_: Campaign, tournament
   134|
   135|**Season Commissioner**:
   136|The **Player** who starts a **Season** and holds season-scoped authority similar to a fantasy sports commissioner.
   137|_Avoid_: Season owner, league manager, commissioner
   138|
   139|**Active Season**:
   140|The current **Season** in a **Group** where **Players** may submit **Jumps** for competition.
   141|_Avoid_: Current season
   142|
   143|**Submission Window**:
   144|The phase of an **Active Season** when **Players** may submit **Jumps** for competition.
   145|_Avoid_: Season window
   146|
   147|**Judging Grace Period**:
   148|The phase after a **Season's** **Submission Window** closes when no new competition **Jumps** may be submitted, but existing **Performed Jumps** may still receive **Judgments**.
   149|_Avoid_: Overtime, judging window extension
   150|
   151|**Finalized Season**:
   152|A **Season** whose **Standings** are locked after the **Judging Grace Period** ends.
   153|_Avoid_: Ended season, closed season, archived season
   154|
   155|**Standings**:
   156|A ranked view of **Players** within a **Group** for a **Season**.
   157|_Avoid_: Leaderboard
   158|
   159|**Award**:
   160|An end-of-**Season** recognition for a notable pattern or achievement, which may be based on total score or a specific scoring factor.
   161|_Avoid_: Badge, achievement
   162|
   163|**House Rules**:
   164|The boundaries that define what behavior can count as valid Supperjumpin play. A **Jump** may be awkward, absurd, or transgressive, but must not require harassment, harmful deception, trespass, unsafe behavior, illegal acts, filming identifiable non-consenting bystanders, or disrupting a business.
   165|_Avoid_: Safety policy, moderation policy
   166|
   167|**Evidence**:
   168|Material submitted to support that a **Jump** happened as claimed, such as photos, video, location, receipts, captions, or witnesses. **Evidence** may inform social judgment or later structured scoring.
   169|_Avoid_: Proof, verification
   170|
   171|**Caption**:
   172|A **Player's** written context for **Evidence**, explaining what happened and why the **Jump** should count.
   173|_Avoid_: Description, note
   174|
   175|**Commitment**:
   176|A scoring factor for how logistically hard or socially awkward a **Jump** is to perform.
   177|_Avoid_: Hardness
   178|
   179|**Transgression**:
   180|A scoring factor for how strongly a **Jump** violates an expected food or place boundary without becoming harassment, trespass, or harm.
   181|_Avoid_: Rule-breaking, illegality
   182|
   183|**Creativity**:
   184|A scoring factor for how novel, thematic, poetic, or absurdly elegant a **Jump** is.
   185|_Avoid_: Originality
   186|
   187|**Presentation**:
   188|A scoring factor for how compellingly the **Evidence** captures the **Jump** as a performance.
   189|_Avoid_: Media quality
   190|
   191|**Credibility**:
   192|How well **Evidence** supports that a **Jump** happened as claimed. **Credibility** is distinct from **Presentation**: a **Jump** may be compellingly documented without being fully believable.
   193|_Avoid_: Truth, proof
   194|
   195|## Example Dialogue
   196|
   197|**Player A**: I have an Idea: Taco Bell in the Olive Garden parking lot.
   198|
   199|**Player B**: That sounds like an Off-Season Jump unless we start a Season first.
   200|
   201|**Player A**: I'll start a Season, so I become the Season Commissioner. Then I'll make it a Planned Jump with Taco Bell as the Source, Olive Garden as the Destination, and a Crunchwrap as the Food.
   202|
   203|**Player C**: Submit photo Evidence and a Caption when you perform it. Once it is a Performed Jump, the Judging Window starts.
   204|
   205|**Player B**: I'll Judge it on Commitment, Transgression, Creativity, and Presentation. If the Evidence looks staged, I may lower Credibility or raise a Dispute.
   206|
   207|**Season Commissioner**: If the Dispute holds, I can mark it as a Disqualified Jump so it stays visible but does not affect Standings.
