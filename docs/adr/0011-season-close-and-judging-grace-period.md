# Season Close and Judging Grace Period

A Season closes in phases: first its Submission Window closes, then a Judging Grace Period allows existing Performed Stunts to receive Judgments, and finally the Season is finalized with locked Standings. Each Stunt has its own Judging Window, but Season finalization is the outer deadline; this allows buzzer-beater Stunts near the end of a Season without letting unresolved Judgments change Standings indefinitely.

Note: The Author Grace Period, a 10-minute window after a Jump is submitted during which the performer may edit or retract, is a per-Jump concept separate from the Season's Judging Grace Period. The Author Grace Period is enforced on the public feed; the Season's Judging Grace Period applies only within a Group Season context. In v1, only the Author Grace Period is implemented; the Season Judging Grace Period is a v2 feature.
