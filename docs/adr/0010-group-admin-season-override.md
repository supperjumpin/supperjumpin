# Group Admin Season Override

⚠️ **Moot per ADR-0035 and ADR-0036.** Seasons and the old Group/Admin model no longer exist; the Community model (forthcoming) replaces it. Preserved as historical record.

Group Admins have emergency override authority over Active Seasons because the Group is the durable social container and a Season is temporary. Season Commissioners own ordinary Season management, but Group Admins may intervene in cases such as abandoned Seasons, abusive disqualifications, or other House Rules disputes; override actions should be visible rather than silently rewriting Season history.
