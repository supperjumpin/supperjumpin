# Progression Separate from Season Competition

⚠️ **Moot per ADR-0035 and ADR-0036.** Seasons, Missions, Bounties, and Levels are cut from the MVP. Preserved as historical record.

Missions, Bounties, Sponsored Bounties, invite rewards, and Player Levels are non-competitive progression systems and do not affect Season Score or Standings by default. Season competition remains based on non-disqualified Judged Stunts in a Group, preventing growth mechanics or sponsor-funded rewards from distorting competitive results.
