# Season Commissioner

Any Player in a Group may start a Season when the Group has no Active Season, and that Player becomes the Season Commissioner. The Season Commissioner may end the Season, assign Awards, resolve House Rules disputes for Season-linked Stunts, and disqualify Stunts from that Season, but does not gain broader Group authority such as removing Players, editing other Players' Evidence, or changing submitted Judgments invisibly.

## MVP Status

Groups and Seasons are deferred from MVP. Season Commissioner is not built at launch. The Open (ADR-0023) is the only competition structure at MVP and is platform-run with no Commissioner role.

The long-term viability of the Season Commissioner model has not been validated. Before Groups and Seasons are implemented, this decision should be regrilled against whatever is learned from the Open.
